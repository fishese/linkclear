LinkClear cleans the URL in your shared text, keeps your words, and opens Android’s share sheet so you can send it on.

Android 8 or newer · Free · Open source

One stop on the way to sharing

  1. Share a link from your usual app.
  2. Choose LinkClear.
  3. Choose where to send the cleaned text.

Preview is off by default. Turn it on in the app if you’d like to review the result first. You can also paste and copy links directly.

More than removing a question mark

Built-in support for Instagram, Threads, Twitter/X and Google share links. Short links are looked up to find their destination. Add or edit site rules in the app, with an optional browser for pages that require login.

Headings, captions and “Source:” text stay intact. Unrecognized content passes through unchanged; failed short-link lookups offer a retry.

Small app, clear boundaries

No LinkClear account, analytics or share history. Direct links are cleaned on your phone. Online resolution contacts the link provider; the optional login browser keeps its own site data until you clear it.

The website is a download page. Link cleaning and resolution happen in the Android app.
